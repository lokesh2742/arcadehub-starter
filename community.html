GOOD GAMES, GOOD SPORT

Community Guidelines

ArcadeHub should be welcoming and fun. These guidelines apply to player names, private rooms and interactions connected with the site.

Be respectful

Do not use hateful, discriminatory, threatening, sexually explicit or harassing names or behaviour. Treat other players as people, even when a match gets competitive.

Play fairly

Do not cheat, exploit bugs to spoil matches, manipulate room state or interfere with another player's ability to play.

Protect privacy

Do not use a display name to reveal someone else's personal information. Share room codes only with people you intend to invite. Never ask another player for passwords, payment details or sensitive information.

Don't spam or abuse the service

Do not flood rooms, automate disruptive activity, distribute malicious links or attempt to overload the website.

Report a problem

If you encounter abuse or a technical problem, report it through the Contact page. Include only the information needed to explain the issue; the public issue tracker should not contain private details.

Enforcement

ArcadeHub may investigate reported misuse and may restrict access to features or rooms where reasonably necessary to protect the service and its players.
