YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy

This page explains what ArcadeHub currently stores and how its browser games use information. It is written for the features presently in the site; it will be updated if new services are added.

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Important: ArcadeHub does not currently offer paid memberships or display advertising. Google AdSense is a future plan, not an active feature. This policy will need another update before advertising or additional analytics are switched on.

Information used by ArcadeHub

  • Player display name: the name you enter is saved in your browser's local storage under arcadehubPlayerName so the site can remember it on that device. It is also sent to a multiplayer room when you join one.
  • Anonymous Firebase identifier: online rooms use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to identify a browser session/player. You do not create a password-based account.
  • Multiplayer room data: Firebase Realtime Database stores room codes, player names/identifiers, game board or card state, turns, scores and room status so the other player can see the match update.
  • Technical data: your browser and hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP address, device/browser details and request logs, to deliver and secure the website.

Where information is stored

The display name is stored locally in your browser until you clear the site's local storage or change the name. Multiplayer room data is stored in the ArcadeHub Firebase Realtime Database. Room records may remain in the database after a match; the current site does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period.

How information is used

Information is used to remember your chosen display name, identify players in online rooms, synchronise games between devices, maintain site functionality and help protect the service from misuse. ArcadeHub does not sell personal information.

Sharing

When you join a multiplayer room, the other participant can see the display name and game information needed to play. Firebase, operated by Google, provides authentication and database infrastructure and processes information to provide those services under its own terms and privacy documentation.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current ArcadeHub application code uses browser local storage for the display name. The Firebase services or hosting environment may use technical storage or process connection data. A Google Analytics measurement ID is present in the Firebase project configuration, but the current site code does not load Google Analytics. No AdSense ad tags are currently installed. If analytics or advertising technologies are enabled later, this policy and any required consent choices will be updated before launch.

Your choices

  • You can change your display name using the Profile control.
  • You can clear browser site data/local storage to remove the name saved on that device.
  • You can choose not to use online multiplayer rooms.
  • For a request about information stored in Firebase, contact the project through the Contact page. Do not include passwords, private room codes or sensitive personal details in a public report.

Children and age

ArcadeHub is a general gaming website and is not designed to collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Policy changes

This policy may change when features, service providers or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.
