THE ARCADEHUB STORY

Play more. Have fun.

ArcadeHub is a browser-based gaming space built to make quick games easy to discover and fun to share. Open the site, choose a game, and jump straight into the action.

Quick solo games

Take a break with arcade and puzzle favourites, including Snake, 2048, Block Puzzle and Neon Racer.

Play together

Challenge a friend in Tic Tac Toe or Memory Match using online private rooms, or play Tic Tac Toe on the same device.

Built for the browser

No desktop game installation is needed. ArcadeHub is designed to work on modern computers and mobile browsers.

What we are building

ArcadeHub is an evolving project. We plan to improve the game library, mobile experience and player features over time. Games marked “Coming soon” are not playable yet.
