Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers to help you get playing.

How do I start a game?

Open the game library, choose a game card and follow the on-screen instructions.

Which games support online multiplayer?

Tic Tac Toe and Memory Match currently support online private rooms. Tic Tac Toe also has VS AI and same-device VS Player modes. Snake, 2048, Block Puzzle and Neon Racer are single-player games.

How do I invite a friend?

Choose the online room option, create a room and share its six-character code with your friend. They should enter that code using the Join room option. Keep the game open while waiting for them to join.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, use a modern mobile browser. Some games use keyboard controls on desktop and touch controls on mobile. Keep your browser updated for the best experience.

How do I change my player name?

Use the Profile button near the top of the home page, edit your display name and save it. The name is remembered on that browser/device.

Why can't my friend join my room?

Check that the code has six characters, both devices have an internet connection, and the room is still waiting for a player. If the room is already full or closed, create a new room and share the new code.

Are all games available now?

No. Games marked “Coming soon” are previews of planned additions and cannot be played yet.

Does ArcadeHub show ads or charge money?

Not at present. The current site is free to use and does not have AdSense ads or paid memberships enabled.

How do I report a bug?

Visit the Contact page and open a GitHub issue with the page/game name, what you expected, what happened and your browser/device. Do not post private information or room codes.
